Join the lab

COSI Lab studies hip and knee surgery using clinical data, imaging, biomechanical modeling, and surgical robotics. We welcome students, orthopaedic trainees, and researchers interested in this work.

How to apply

Review our current research, then use the application form to tell us about your background and the work you are interested in.

Who can apply

Undergraduates

Students interested in clinical or computational orthopaedic research.

Medical students

Students interested in research on hip and knee surgery, including clinical outcomes and surgical technology.

Orthopaedic residents and fellows

Trainees with clinical research questions in outcomes, biomechanics, or surgical robotics.

Postdoctoral researchers and collaborators

Researchers in machine learning, biomechanics, statistics, and related fields.
