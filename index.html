The future of orthopaedics is computational

COSI is a research lab led by Professor Stefano A. Bini, MD within the UCSF Department of Orthopaedic Surgery. We use machine learning, population-scale clinical data, biomechanical simulation, and surgical robotics to change how hip and knee surgery is studied, planned, and performed.
